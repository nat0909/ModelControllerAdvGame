Max Hit Points: when it reaches 0, the creature dies
Dexterity: subtracks from the accuracy of attacks against the creature (makes them harder to hit)

Melee Accuracy: added to the roll to hit another creature with a melee attack
Melee Damage: damage melee attacks inflict on other creatures

Ranged Accuracy: added to the roll to hit another creature with a ranged attack
Ranged Damage: damage ranged attacks inflict on other creatures
